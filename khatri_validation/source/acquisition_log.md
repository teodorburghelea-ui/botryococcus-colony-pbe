# Khatri et al. (2014) acquisition log

The complete 11-page article PDF was located in the project archive at `Comparison_Literature/Comparison_Khatri/` and copied to `source/Khatri_2014.pdf`. The PDF metadata identifies *Biotechnology and Bioengineering* 111:493--503 (2014), DOI 10.1002/bit.25126.

Figure 6 is on printed page 499. The horizontal bars encode colony diameter on the upper 0.0--0.5 mm axis; the dashed diamonds encode optical density on the lower axis and were not used as colony diameters. Four bar endpoints were digitized from a 300 dpi rendering. The paper reports 40--150 aggregate images per condition and standard-deviation bars. The digitized values are approximate graph extractions and retain the original 5--12.5% labeled replacement conditions. The paper text also describes a 15% condition as close to washout, but Figure 6 does not show a corresponding colony-diameter bar; it is therefore excluded from the quantitative digitization.
